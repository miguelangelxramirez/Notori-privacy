# Notori Support

Updated 30 September 2026

## What Notori is

Notori is an AI notes app. You save ideas, links, photos and PDFs; Notori connects related notes into Topics and answers your questions with cited sources from your own notes. AI is an essential part of Notori and you accept its processing before you start. It works through Notori's server and OpenAI, as described in the [Privacy Policy](../privacy-policy/).

## Manage or cancel your subscription

Notori requires an active subscription or, if you are eligible, a seven-day free trial. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To change or cancel one, open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Notori. You can also use Settings, then Manage subscription, inside Notori. To ask for a refund, use Apple's "Report a Problem" page at reportaproblem.apple.com, because Apple handles the billing.

## Export your notes

In Notori, open Settings and tap "Export my notes" to save all your notes as a Markdown file.

## Delete your data

In Notori, open Settings and tap "Delete all my data". Notori deletes every note and all derived data from your device and asks iCloud to delete your private iCloud copy. If iCloud is unavailable at that moment, sign in to iCloud and run the action again. Deleting your data does not cancel your subscription; cancel it in Apple's subscription settings.

## iCloud

With iCloud turned on, your notes sync through your own private iCloud to your other devices. You can manage iCloud for Notori from Settings on your device, under your name, iCloud.

# Notori Privacy Policy

Updated 12 September 2026

## Privacy at a glance

Notori is designed so that note and local search work on your iPhone without an account. AI is integral to Notori and accepting its processing is required before using the app. Notori does not use advertising trackers.

## 1. Data stored on your device

Your notes, links, edits, derived topics and relationships, local search index, settings, AI-consent choice and widget snapshot are stored in Notori’s private app or App Group container. The current vNext experience does not sync this database through a Notori account. Apple may include app data in device backups according to your iCloud or backup settings.

## 2. AI processing

Only after you consent, note text, your questions and a bounded amount of relevant source content may be sent to Notori’s Cloudflare Worker and then to OpenAI to organise material or answer your question. For photos you save, a resized copy of the image is also sent with its recognised text to describe objects and scenes, not just words. The uploaded copy is generated without the original file metadata; the original photo remains stored on your device. This requires accepting the updated notice even if you previously accepted text processing. Requests are protected by device attestation, an installation identifier, rate limits and size limits. The Worker requests store:false. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default; unless a stricter retention arrangement applies, abuse-monitoring logs may retain content for up to 30 days. Do not send information you do not want processed by these providers. There is no setting to turn AI off or use Notori in a local-only mode. Stop using Notori if you no longer accept this processing.

## 3. Security identifier

Notori creates a persistent random installation identifier and uses Apple App Attest signals to defend the AI service, enforce limits and investigate abuse. The identifier may be associated with AI requests, but is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## 4. Purchases

Apple processes payment information. RevenueCat receives an anonymous persistent app-user identifier plus subscription and purchase status so Notori can offer, restore and manage Premium access, analyse purchase reliability and prevent fraud. Notori does not receive your full payment-card details. This data is not used for advertising tracking.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Items moved to Notori’s trash remain on the device until you restore them or remove the app data; there is currently no automatic trash purge. Removing the app and its data removes the local database from that device. Copies may remain in Apple backups until those backups expire or you delete them. Operational security records held by service providers follow their own retention periods, including the OpenAI period described above.

## 6. Sharing and legal requests

Notori shares data only with the processors described above when needed to provide features, protect the service, comply with law, or complete a transaction. Notori does not sell personal data.

## 7. Your choices and contact

You can stop using Notori, manage subscriptions through Apple, and remove local data from your device. Stopping use does not change the retention periods described above. For privacy requests or questions, use the support contact linked from Notori’s App Store page. This notice should be read together with the final public privacy policy for your country or region.
